# CLAUDE.md

Personal developer portfolio (React 18 + TypeScript + Vite + Tailwind + Framer Motion). Content lives in `src/data/`, theme colors in `src/index.css` CSS variables.

## Evaluation lens (STRICT)

Judge every change, suggestion, and review from a **portfolio / hiring / ATS perspective first**, code quality second:

- Does it help a recruiter or hiring manager understand who Waqas is, what he builds, and why to hire him, within ~10 seconds?
- Does it give proof (shipped projects, measurable impact, real experience) and not just claims or decoration?
- Is it consistent with the resume (titles, dates, companies, stack), with no contradicting or inflated stats?
- Is the content ATS and SEO friendly: plain-text keywords, semantic HTML, meta/OG tags, a downloadable résumé?
- Do the visuals support the content? Cut effects that read as template or AI slop.

## Working style

- Keep answers short and to the point.
- Never invent experience, metrics, or projects. Ask instead.
